S.A.A.N. LIGTAS?

A Web-Based School Hazard Awareness and Interactive Evacuation Mapping Platform for Emergency Preparedness, developed for Alangilan Senior High School in Batangas City, Philippines. The name asks a simple question every learner should be able to answer: saan ligtas? (where is it safe?).

What It Does

  • Shows an interactive evacuation map
  • Explains safety protocols
  • Guides emergency preparedness
  • Provides learning resources
  • Offers quizzes to test knowledge
  • Lists emergency information

For Whom

Made for the whole school community:

  • Students — learn what to do and where to go
  • Teachers — guide classes during drills and emergencies
  • School staff — support safe evacuation and response

Hazard-Prone Areas

Places on campus that may be affected by earthquakes, fire, flooding, or crowding. They will be marked in red once the school identifies them.

Evacuation Routes & Assembly Areas

Colored arrows show each floor's way out. Safe assembly areas will be marked in green once the school confirms them.

Warning & Information Points

Red warning icons mark emergency points such as first aid. Blue icons mark information points such as gates.

Map & School Details

Alangilan Senior High School, Batangas City. Based on the school's evacuation plan for the campus buildings.
